;Task:
Implement the basic primitives of '''Continuum Computing''' - a computational paradigm
suited for analog AI, control systems, perception, physical simulation, signal reasoning,
semantic manifolds, and recursive self-models.

;Core Operations:

1. '''bind''' (⊗): a ⊗ b - Spectral entanglement via Kronecker product coupling.
   Creates composite states from independent quantum-like states.

2. '''route''' (e^(iθ)): Phase rotation as address/path transformation.
   Rotates state vectors in complex space to change information routing.

3. '''compute''' (*): a * b - Convolution as transformation.
   Applies filters/kernels for signal transformation and pattern matching.

4. '''decide''': argmax_k |⟨Ψ, Φ_k⟩| - Readout by resonance against basis patterns.
   Selects the basis vector with maximum correlation to the state.

;Mathematical Foundation:

* '''Spectral Entanglement (Kronecker/Tensor)''': (A ⊗ B)_{(i-1)q+k, (j-1)s+l} = A_{ij} × B_{kl}
* '''Phase Rotation''': Ψ' = e^(iθ) Ψ where θ can be a scalar or matrix
* '''Convolution''': (f * g)[n] = Σ_m f[m] g[n-m]  
* '''Resonance Decision''': k* = argmax_k |⟨Ψ, Φ_k⟩| = argmax_k |Σ_j Ψ_j × conj(Φ_kj)|

<pre>

</pre>

<pre>

⟨Ψ, Φ_0⟩ = 0.707
⟨Ψ, Φ_1⟩ = 0.707
Result: Either 0 or 1 (tie-break by first)
</pre>
